## {++sectionNumber}. Introduction

Welcome to {siteData.title}. We respect your privacy. This policy explains
what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and
the choices you have.

## {++sectionNumber}. Information We Collect

- **What you send us:** when you fill out a form, the details you enter: your
  name, phone number, email, company, message and project details, plus the
  page you sent it from.
- **Basic technical data:** like any website, our host receives your IP
  address and browser details with each page request.
- **Visit counts:** we count page visits and measure page speed without
  cookies and without identifying you.

We don't ask for payment details, Social Security numbers or other sensitive
information through this website.

## {++sectionNumber}. How We Use Your Information

- To call, text or email you back about your request, as you agreed to on the form
- To give you an estimate and do the work you ask for
- To keep the website secure and working, and to improve it

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## {++sectionNumber}. How Long We Keep It

Form submissions stay in our email so we can follow up on your project and
keep records of our work. Ask us and we'll delete yours, unless we need to
keep it for legal or business records.

## {++sectionNumber}. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your
information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or
destruction. No method of sending data over the internet is 100% secure.
