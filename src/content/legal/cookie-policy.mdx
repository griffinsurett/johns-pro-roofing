## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Browser
storage works the same way. Websites use them to remember things between
visits.

## Page Visit Counts

We count page visits and measure page speed with Vercel Web Analytics and
Speed Insights. They don't use cookies, don't identify you, and don't
follow you to other websites.

## Managing Your Choices

Use **Your Privacy Choices** at the bottom of any page to change your cookie
settings. You can also clear or block cookies and browser storage in your
browser settings. The site still works without them, but it won't remember
your accessibility settings.
