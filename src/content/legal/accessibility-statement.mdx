{siteData.legalName} is committed to making our website accessible to people
with disabilities. We want every visitor to be able to learn about our
services, contact us and request an estimate.

## Scope

This statement covers the {siteData.title} website and requests for help
getting information about our services.

## Our Accessibility Goal

Our goal is to work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2,
Level AA. This describes what we're aiming for. It is not a certification
that every part of the website currently meets that standard.

## Request Assistance or Report a Problem

If you have difficulty using our website, call us or use our contact form.
Tell us which page you were on, what you were trying to do and the problem
you ran into. You can also tell us your browser or assistive technology if
that helps. Please don't send payment details or medical records.

We'll review your request and work with you to find an accessible way to get
the information or help you need, including by phone.

## Forms

Our forms are delivered by an outside service, Formspree. If a form doesn't
work for you, call us and we'll take your details over the phone.
