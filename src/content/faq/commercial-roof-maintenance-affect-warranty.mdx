It can. Many commercial roof warranties require documented regular inspections and upkeep, and skipping that can put coverage at risk. Our written maintenance reports give you the paper trail manufacturers often want to see if a claim ever comes up. Check the terms of your specific warranty, and we can align our visits to what it requires.
