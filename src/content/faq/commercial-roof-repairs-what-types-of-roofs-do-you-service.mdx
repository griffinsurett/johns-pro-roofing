We focus on flat and low-slope commercial roofs, which covers most of the systems you'll find on buildings around New Jersey, including single-ply membranes, modified bitumen, and built-up roofs. Because repair methods and materials differ from system to system, we match the patch or seal to what's already on your building so it bonds and lasts. If you're not sure what kind of roof you have, we'll identify it during the free estimate.
