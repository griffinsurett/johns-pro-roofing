Sealcoating on a regular cycle protects the surface from water, UV, and oil, and staying ahead of crack filling keeps water out of the base, which is what actually destroys a lot over time. The right interval depends on your traffic and how the surface is holding up, so we'd rather look at it than throw out a one-size-fits-all number. We're glad to set up a maintenance plan that fits your property and budget.
