We primarily install architectural asphalt shingles, which hold up well in New Jersey's mix of heat, wind, and freeze-thaw winters and come in a range of colors and styles. Architectural shingles are heavier and more durable than the older three-tab style, and most homeowners find they give the best balance of looks, longevity, and cost. We'll walk you through the options during your free estimate so you can pick what fits your home and budget.
