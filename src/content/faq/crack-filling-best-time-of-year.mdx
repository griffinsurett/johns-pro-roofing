Spring through fall is ideal because the sealant bonds best in dry weather and moderate temperatures, and the cracks need to be fully dry to seal properly. The smartest move is to get cracks filled before winter so water can't get in and freeze, which is what turns a crack into a pothole by spring. If you're planning ahead, we can get you on the schedule for the right window.
