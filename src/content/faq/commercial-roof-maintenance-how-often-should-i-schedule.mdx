For most commercial roofs in New Jersey we recommend at least twice a year, typically in the spring and fall, so we can catch damage from winter freeze-thaw cycles and prep for the next season. It's also smart to have us out after any major storm. If your roof is older or has a history of leaks, we may suggest a more frequent schedule.
