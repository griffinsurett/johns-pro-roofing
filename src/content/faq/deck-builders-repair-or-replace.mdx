It depends on the structure underneath. If the footings, framing, and the connection to your house are solid, worn boards, loose railings, and tired stairs can usually be repaired. If the framing is rotted or the deck is pulling away from the house, a replacement is the safer call. We'll inspect it and tell you honestly which one you need.
