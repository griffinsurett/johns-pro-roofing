The main drivers are the size of the damaged area, the type of roofing system you have, how much water has gotten into the underlying layers, and how accessible the roof is. Storm damage that involves several areas or wet insulation will cost more than a single flashing repair. We give free estimates and offer financing, so you'll know the number before any work starts, with no surprises.
