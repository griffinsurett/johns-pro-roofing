Cracks are how water gets under your asphalt, and once it's under there it undermines the base and freezes and thaws through the New Jersey winter until the crack becomes a pothole. Filling cracks early seals that water out and buys your lot a lot more life for a fraction of what a repair or repave costs down the road.
