The biggest thing is keeping water out, so staying on top of crack filling and sealcoating helps protect both the repairs and the rest of the lot. Making sure drainage works and water isn't pooling on the surface goes a long way too, since standing water is what breaks asphalt down. Catching small cracks and soft spots early is far cheaper than waiting for them to become potholes. We're glad to set up a simple maintenance plan so you're addressing problems while they're still small.
