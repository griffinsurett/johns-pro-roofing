Yes — prep is a big part of doing it right. We clean the cracks out of dirt, debris, and loose material and dry them before sealing, because hot-pour sealant only bonds properly to a clean surface. Skipping that step is the main reason cheap crack jobs fail early, so it's always part of ours.
