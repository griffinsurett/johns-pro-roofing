Most straightforward repairs, like a flashing fix or sealing a seam, can be completed in a single day. Larger storm-damage repairs or areas with wet insulation that needs to be removed and replaced can take longer. Once we've inspected the roof, we'll give you a realistic timeline and work around your building's operating hours so we're not in the way of your tenants or staff.
