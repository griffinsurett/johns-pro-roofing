The biggest factors are the square footage of the lot, the condition of the existing asphalt, and how much prep work is needed, such as crack filling, oil-spot priming, and cleaning before we seal. Lots that need striping redone or minor repairs first will run more than a straightforward reseal. We give free, itemized estimates so you can see exactly what's driving the number, and financing is available if you'd rather spread the cost out.
