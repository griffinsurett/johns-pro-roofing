A typical repair starts with inspecting the affected area to find the actual source of the problem, which isn't always where the water shows up inside. From there we address the cause, whether that's resealing seams, replacing flashing, patching the membrane, or removing and replacing wet insulation. We clean up the site when we're done and let you know what we found so you can plan for anything else the roof may need down the road.
