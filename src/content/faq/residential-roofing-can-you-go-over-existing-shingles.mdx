Sometimes it's possible to lay new shingles over a single existing layer, and it can save on labor and disposal, but it isn't always the right call. Going over old shingles hides any decking problems underneath and can shorten the life of the new roof, so we usually recommend a full tear-off when the goal is longevity. We'll inspect what you have and tell you honestly which approach makes sense for your home.
