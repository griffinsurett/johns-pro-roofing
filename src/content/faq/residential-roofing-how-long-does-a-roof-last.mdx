A quality architectural shingle roof that's installed correctly and kept up can last a long time, though the exact lifespan depends on the shingle grade, ventilation, and how much sun and weather it takes. In New Jersey, freeze-thaw cycles and summer heat are the biggest wear factors, and good attic ventilation makes a real difference. Regular checkups and small repairs go a long way toward getting the full life out of your roof.
