A properly done hot-pour crack fill typically holds up for several years, though how long depends on your traffic, drainage, and how the pavement moves through our freeze-thaw winters. It's a maintenance step, not a permanent fix, so pavement naturally develops new cracks over time. We're happy to check it during future visits and touch up anything that's opened back up.
