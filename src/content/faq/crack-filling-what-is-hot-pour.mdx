Hot-pour crack filling uses a rubberized sealant that we heat to a liquid and pour directly into the crack, where it flows down deep and then bonds to both sides as it cools. Because it stays flexible, it moves with the pavement when it expands and contracts through the seasons instead of cracking back open like cheaper cold-pour products tend to. That flexibility is why it holds up better on commercial lots that see constant traffic and temperature swings.
