As a rule of thumb, any crack wide enough to slip a coin or a pencil into is wide enough to let water in and worth sealing. Hairline cracks usually aren't urgent, but long connected cracks, cracks near drains, or ones that are already starting to crumble at the edges should be addressed before winter.
