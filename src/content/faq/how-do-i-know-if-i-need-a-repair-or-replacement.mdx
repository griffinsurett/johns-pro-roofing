category: "General Roofing"
---
If the damage is localized and the underlying deck is sound, a repair is usually the right call. Widespread leaks, saturated insulation, or a roof near the end of its service life often point to replacement. Our free inspection will tell you which makes sense — we won't sell you a roof you don't need.
