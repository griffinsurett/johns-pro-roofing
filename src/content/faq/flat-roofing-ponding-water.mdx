Some water sitting after a storm is normal, but water that stays put for a day or more usually points to drainage issues, low spots, clogged drains, or sagging in the deck or insulation. Left alone, ponding adds weight, speeds up membrane wear, and raises leak risk. We can find the cause and correct the slope, drains, or substrate as part of a repair or replacement.
