If the base underneath is still solid and the damage is mostly surface-level cracking or wear, resurfacing (an overlay) is usually the smart, cost-effective move. But when you're seeing widespread alligator cracking, potholes that keep coming back, or soft spots where the base has failed, an overlay won't hold and full-depth reconstruction is the honest recommendation. We'll core or probe the lot during the free estimate so we're advising you based on what's actually going on, not guesswork.
