New asphalt usually needs a day or so before it can take vehicle traffic, though this stretches in hot weather since fresh asphalt stays soft until it fully cools. It keeps curing and hardening for several weeks after that, so we'll ask you to avoid sharp turns, jacking stands, and heavy point loads early on. We'll give you clear return-to-service timing for your specific job before we wrap up.
