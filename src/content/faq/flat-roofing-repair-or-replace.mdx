If the damage is isolated, the membrane is otherwise sound, and the roof still has years of life left, a repair usually makes sense. When you're seeing repeated leaks in multiple spots, widespread ponding, saturated insulation, or a system near the end of its service life, replacement is often the smarter long-term spend.
