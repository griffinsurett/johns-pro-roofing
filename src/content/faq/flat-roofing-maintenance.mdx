The basics go a long way: keep drains and gutters clear, remove debris, and get regular inspections, especially after big storms and at the change of seasons. Catching small seam splits, punctures, or flashing issues early keeps them from turning into interior leaks and rot. We can set you up with periodic inspections so problems get handled before they get expensive.
