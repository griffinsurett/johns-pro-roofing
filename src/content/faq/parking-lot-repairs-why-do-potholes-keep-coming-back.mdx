A pothole that keeps returning is almost always a sign the problem is under the surface, not on top of it. When water gets into a failed base and freezes and thaws through the winter, a thin surface patch just pops back out. That's why we look at what's underneath before we patch, and recommend full-depth repair where the base is the actual cause. Fixing it right the first time costs more up front but stops you from paying for the same hole every year.
