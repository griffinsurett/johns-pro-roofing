A properly installed lot with a good base can last many years, but the real driver is maintenance: sealcoating, crack filling, and keeping water from getting under the surface. Traffic volume, heavy trucks, and NJ's freeze-thaw cycles all wear a lot faster than most people expect. Stay on top of the small stuff and you push a full replacement much further down the road, which is exactly what we'll help you plan for.
