A properly done repair on a roof that still has good life left in it can hold up for years, especially when we address the real source of the problem rather than just the symptom. How long it lasts depends on the condition of the surrounding membrane, the roof's age, and ongoing maintenance. If your roof is near the end of its service life, we'll tell you honestly so you can weigh repair costs against planning for a replacement.
