Full-depth patching means we saw-cut and remove the damaged asphalt all the way down, address the base underneath, and then repave that section. You need it when the problem goes deeper than the surface, like when a pothole keeps coming back or the base has failed from water or heavy truck traffic. Surface patching over a bad base just fails again, so we recommend full-depth work when the underlying support is the real issue. We'll tell you which approach a given area actually calls for during your free estimate.
