In most cases, yes. We regularly phase commercial jobs so we're working one section at a time, keeping entrances, fire lanes, and enough parking open while a portion cures. For sites that can't spare any downtime during the day, we can schedule around your operating hours, including nights or weekends. We'll map out the phasing with you up front so there are no surprises for your tenants or customers.
