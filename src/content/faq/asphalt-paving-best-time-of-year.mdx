Asphalt lays and compacts best in warm, dry weather, so late spring through early fall is the sweet spot here in NJ. It can be done outside that window when conditions allow, but cold ground and rain make good compaction harder and can affect the finished result. If timing matters for your property, reach out early and we'll get you on the schedule for the right conditions.
