A typical project covers inspecting and prepping the roof, any needed tear-off, deck and insulation repair, installing the new membrane system with proper flashing and detailing around penetrations and drains, and full cleanup when we're done. We walk the finished roof with you so you know exactly what was done. Your free estimate spells out the scope line by line before any work starts, so there are no surprises.
