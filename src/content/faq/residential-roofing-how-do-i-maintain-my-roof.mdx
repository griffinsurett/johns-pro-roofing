The basics go a long way: keep your gutters clear, trim back overhanging branches, and have the roof looked at after major storms. Watch for loose or missing shingles, and address small leaks or flashing issues before they spread. If you'd rather not climb up there yourself, we're glad to come out and take a look, and catching the little things early is what keeps a small repair from turning into a big one.
