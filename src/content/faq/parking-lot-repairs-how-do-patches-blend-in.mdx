New patches start out darker than the surrounding asphalt and gradually blend closer as they age and oxidize, though a repaired section will usually still be visible up close. We square off and compact the edges cleanly so the repair is solid and holds up, not just so it looks tidy. If you want a more uniform appearance across the whole lot, sealcoating after the repairs is a good option, and we're happy to talk through that during your estimate.
