Sealcoating puts a protective layer over your asphalt that shields it from water, UV rays, and chemicals like oil and gas that break pavement down over time. It slows oxidation and cracking, and it restores that clean, uniform black finish that makes a property look maintained. Think of it as regular upkeep that extends the life of the asphalt you already paid for rather than a repair. We're happy to walk your lot and give you a free estimate.
