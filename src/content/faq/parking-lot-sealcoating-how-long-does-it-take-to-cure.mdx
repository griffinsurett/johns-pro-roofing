Sealcoat needs time to cure before traffic goes back on it, and the exact window depends on temperature, humidity, and sun that day. In good weather a lot is often ready for traffic by the next day, but we always give you the real timeline for your job based on conditions. We'll confirm reopening times up front so you can plan tenant and customer access around it.
