Cost mostly comes down to the square footage involved, how deep the repair has to go, and how many separate areas need attention. Full-depth patching costs more than a surface pothole fix because there's more removal, base work, and material. Access, traffic control, and how much of the lot we can work on at once also play a role. We give free, itemized estimates so you can see exactly what's driving the number, and we offer financing if you'd rather spread the work out.
