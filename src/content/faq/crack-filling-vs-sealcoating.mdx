They do two different jobs. Crack filling seals the actual cracks so water can't get down into the base, while sealcoating is a thin protective coating spread over the whole surface to guard against sun, oil, and wear. Crack filling comes first — you fill the cracks, then sealcoat over top — and together they're the core of keeping a lot in good shape.
