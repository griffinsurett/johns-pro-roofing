For most buildings a routine inspection and cleaning takes a few hours, though a larger roof or one with lots of rooftop equipment can run longer. If we find minor issues to fix on the spot, that may add a bit of time, and we'll let you know. We'll give you an expected window when we schedule so you can plan around it.
