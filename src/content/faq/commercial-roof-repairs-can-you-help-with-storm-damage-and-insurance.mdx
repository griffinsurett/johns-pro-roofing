Yes, storm damage is a big part of what we repair, from wind-lifted membrane and torn flashing to leaks that show up after heavy rain. We can document the damage with photos and provide a detailed estimate that you can submit to your insurance carrier. While the claim is ultimately between you and your insurer, we're glad to give you the information you need and can start with an emergency stabilization if water is actively getting in.
