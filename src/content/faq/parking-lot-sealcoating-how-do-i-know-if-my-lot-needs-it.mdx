The clearest sign is color: if your asphalt has faded from black to gray, oxidation has started and the surface is losing its protection. Other signs include water soaking in instead of beading, fine surface cracking, and a rough, raveling texture where loose stone comes up. If you're not sure, we'll come out and look at it for free and tell you honestly whether it's time or whether you can wait.
