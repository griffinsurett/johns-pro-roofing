A handful of pothole patches can often be done in a single day, while multiple full-depth sections take longer because of the removal and base work involved. The bigger factor is usually how much of your lot we can close off at one time without shutting down your business. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate and schedule the work in phases if you need to keep the lot open.
