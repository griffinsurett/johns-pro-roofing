Fresh asphalt patches need time to cool and set before traffic goes over them, and how long depends on the weather, the depth of the repair, and the temperature that day. We'll give you clear guidance on each area before we leave so you don't drive on it too soon. In warmer weather it sets up faster, and we'll coordinate the timing so you can reopen those spaces as quickly as it's safe to.
