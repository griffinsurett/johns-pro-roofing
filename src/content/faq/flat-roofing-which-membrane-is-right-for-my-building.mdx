It depends on your building's roof size, slope, foot traffic, budget, and what's already up there. TPO is a popular choice for its reflectivity and energy savings, EPDM holds up well for durability, and modified bitumen and built-up systems are strong options for certain low-slope conditions. We'll come out, look at your actual roof, and give you a straight recommendation with a free estimate rather than pushing one product for every job.
