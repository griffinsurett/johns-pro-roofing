Often, yes, but standing water is usually a grading or drainage issue, and just paving over it without correcting the slope will bring the puddles right back. Recurring potholes tend to mean water has gotten into the base, so we look at what's causing it rather than only patching the surface. During the free estimate we'll assess the drainage and recommend the fix that actually solves the problem, whether that's regrading, base repair, or full reconstruction. Financing is available if the right fix is a bigger project than you planned for.
