A proper job starts with cleaning the lot so the sealer bonds to the asphalt, then treating oil and grease spots so they don't bleed through. We fill cracks, prime any bare or worn areas, apply the sealcoat, and let it cure before reopening. If you want striping and parking stall lines refreshed, we handle that once the surface is dry. We'll lay out the exact scope for your lot in the estimate.
