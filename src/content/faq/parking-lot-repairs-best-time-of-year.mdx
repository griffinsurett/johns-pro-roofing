Warmer, dry weather is ideal for asphalt work because the material lays and cures better, so spring through fall is the sweet spot in New Jersey. That said, small failed sections and safety hazards like deep potholes shouldn't wait, and we can handle urgent repairs in colder months when needed. If a repair can reasonably wait for better conditions, we'll tell you. Getting on the schedule early in the season is the best way to avoid the busy-weather backlog.
