A properly installed and maintained flat roof can serve you for a long service life, though the exact span varies by system, climate exposure, and how well it's kept up. TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, and built-up roofs each age differently, and regular maintenance makes a real difference in how many years you get. We can assess the system you have and give you an honest read on where it stands.
