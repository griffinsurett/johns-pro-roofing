In most cases, no. Since the work happens on the roof, your team can usually keep running as normal, and we're happy to schedule around your busiest hours or come in early to stay out of the way. If a repair involves anything noisy or near an occupied space, we'll coordinate the timing with you ahead of time. Just let us know your operating hours when we set up the visit.
