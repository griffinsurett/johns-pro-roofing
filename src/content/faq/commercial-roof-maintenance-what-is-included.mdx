A typical visit includes a full inspection of the membrane or roof surface, flashings, seams, penetrations, drains, and rooftop equipment curbs. We clear debris from drains and gutters, reseal or repair minor issues we find on the spot when it makes sense, and give you a written report with photos of anything that needs attention. The goal is to catch small problems before they turn into leaks or bigger repairs.
