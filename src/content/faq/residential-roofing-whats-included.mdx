A full replacement includes tearing off the old roofing down to the decking, inspecting and repairing the decking as needed, and installing new underlayment, flashing, and shingles. We also handle the details that keep water out over time, like proper valley and drip-edge work, and we clean up the site and haul away the old material when we're done. Your written estimate spells out exactly what's covered so there are no surprises.
