Cost comes down mostly to the total linear footage of cracks, how wide and deep they are, and how much prep the lot needs before we can seal. A lot with a few clean cracks is a quick job; one with heavy, crumbling cracking takes more material and labor. We give free estimates so you get a real number for your specific lot, and we offer financing if you'd rather spread the cost out.
