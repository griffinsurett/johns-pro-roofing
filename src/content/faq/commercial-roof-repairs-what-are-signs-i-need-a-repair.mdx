Common warning signs include water stains on interior ceilings, bubbling or blistering in the membrane, standing water that doesn't drain, cracked or lifting flashing, and higher-than-usual energy bills. On a flat roof, problems often show up around drains, seams, and rooftop penetrations like vents and HVAC curbs. If you're seeing any of these, it's worth having us take a look before a small issue turns into interior damage.
