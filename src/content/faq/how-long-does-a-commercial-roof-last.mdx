---
title: "How long does a commercial roof last?"
description: "How long does a commercial roof last?"
publishDate: 2026-01-01
order: 11
category: "General Roofing"
---
It depends on the system and how well it's maintained. A quality flat-roof membrane typically lasts 20–30 years, and metal or restored/coated roofs can last even longer. Regular maintenance is the single biggest factor in getting the full life out of any roof.
