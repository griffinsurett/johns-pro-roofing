Sealcoat cures best in warm, dry weather, so in New Jersey the season generally runs from late spring through early fall. It needs consistently warmer temperatures and no rain in the cure window, which is why we watch the forecast closely and schedule accordingly. If you reach out early we can get you on the calendar for the right stretch of weather rather than rushing a job in marginal conditions.
