Most single-family asphalt shingle roofs are a one to two day job once we start, weather permitting. Larger homes, steep roofs, or unexpected decking repairs can add time, and we won't rush a job at the cost of doing it right. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your estimate and keep you posted if New Jersey weather forces us to adjust the schedule.
