It depends on the size of the lot and the scope, but many resurfacing jobs are a day or two of active work, while full-depth reconstruction takes longer because of excavation, base prep, and compaction. We'll give you a realistic schedule in writing before we start, and we can phase the work or handle it in sections so your operation keeps running. Weather is the wild card here in NJ, so we build a little flexibility into the plan.
