Keeping the lot clean goes a long way: sweep off sand and debris, clean up oil and fluid leaks promptly, and address any new cracks before water gets into them. Good drainage matters too, since standing water is hard on any pavement. Handle the small stuff and your surface stays protected longer between reseals, and we're glad to check in periodically and let you know when it's time for the next coat.
