Sealcoating is a protective surface coating, not a structural repair, so it won't fill potholes or close large cracks on its own. We handle crack filling and spot repairs as prep before we seal, but if the asphalt has serious potholes or failed areas, those need patching or repaving first. When we look at your lot we'll tell you honestly whether it's ready to seal or whether some repair work should come first.
