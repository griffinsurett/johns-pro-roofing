Yes. Active leaks during or after a storm can't always wait, so we handle emergency commercial repairs to stop water from getting into your building and damaging inventory, equipment, or interior finishes. In many cases we can perform a temporary stabilization first to control the leak, then schedule the permanent repair. Call us as soon as you notice water coming in so we can get out to you quickly.
