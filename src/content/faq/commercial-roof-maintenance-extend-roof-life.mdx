Yes. Small issues like open seams, failing sealant, and blocked drainage are what shorten a roof's usable life, and they're cheap to fix when caught early. Staying on a maintenance schedule lets you get the full service life out of the roof you already paid for instead of replacing it years ahead of schedule. It's one of the best returns you can get on a commercial property.
