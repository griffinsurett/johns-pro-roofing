Timing depends on the roof's size, the system being installed, and the weather, but many commercial flat roofs are completed in a few days to a couple of weeks. Tear-off, deck or insulation repairs, and complex rooftop equipment can add time. We'll give you a realistic schedule up front and keep you posted if conditions change.
