We maintain the flat and low-slope systems common on New Jersey commercial buildings, including TPO, EPDM, PVC, modified bitumen, and built-up roofs, as well as metal roofing. Each system has its own wear points and repair methods, so our crews adjust the inspection to match what you have. If you're not sure what's on your building, we can identify it during a free assessment.
