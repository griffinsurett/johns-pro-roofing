Here in New Jersey, the milder, drier stretches of spring through fall tend to be ideal, since many membrane systems adhere and seal best in moderate temperatures and dry conditions. That said, roofs don't always fail on a convenient schedule, and we can handle work in colder months by adjusting materials and methods. If you're planning ahead, reach out early so we can get you on the calendar for the best window.
