Most commercial lots benefit from resealing every few years, but the right interval depends on how much traffic you get, whether you have heavy trucks or delivery vehicles, and how much sun and standing water the surface sees. A high-turnover retail lot wears faster than a lightly used office lot. When we do your estimate we'll give you a realistic schedule for your specific property so you're not sealing too early or waiting too long.
