It usually comes down to how much of the roof is affected and its overall condition. Isolated leaks, split seams, or damaged flashing on an otherwise sound flat or low-slope roof are typically good repair candidates, while widespread saturation or a membrane at the end of its life often points toward replacement. We'll come out for a free estimate, inspect the whole system, and give you a straight recommendation either way, not just push the bigger job.
