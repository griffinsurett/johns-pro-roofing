There's noise and activity while we're working, and a tear-off does create debris, but we protect your landscaping and siding and clean up thoroughly at the end of each day. We use tarps and catch systems to keep nails and shingle scraps out of your yard, and we do a magnetic sweep before we leave. You can stay home during the work, and we'll do our best to keep the disruption to your day as short as possible.
