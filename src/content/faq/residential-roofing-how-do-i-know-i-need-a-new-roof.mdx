It depends on the age of the roof and how widespread the damage is. A few missing or cracked shingles, a small leak, or storm damage in one area can usually be repaired, but if you're seeing curling shingles, granules filling your gutters, or leaks in multiple spots on an older roof, replacement is often the smarter long-term move. The best way to know is to have us take a look. We offer free estimates and will give you an honest read, not push you toward a replacement you don't need.
