The biggest cost drivers are the roof's square footage, the membrane system you choose, the condition of what's underneath, and how much tear-off, insulation, or deck repair is needed. Access, roof penetrations, drains, and code-required upgrades also factor in. Every roof is different, so we give a free, itemized estimate after we inspect it, and we offer financing if you'd rather spread the investment out.
