## What drives the cost

The size of the room matters, but it's rarely the biggest factor. These usually are:

- Moving plumbing, gas lines or walls. In a kitchen, keeping the sink, stove and fridge where they are is the biggest single way to keep the cost down.
- The cabinets, countertops, tile and fixtures you choose
- What's behind the walls once they're opened up, like old wiring or water damage
- Whether it's one room or several

We'll show you where the money's going during your free estimate, and we'll tell you where you can save without cutting corners.
