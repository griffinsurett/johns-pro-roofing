## Kitchen remodeling

The kitchen is the room you use most, so a remodel there has to work every day, not just look good. A kitchen remodel can include:

- A new layout that improves how you move and cook
- Cabinets, countertops, and backsplash
- Flooring and lighting
- Plumbing and electrical changes for a new layout or appliances

The biggest cost decision is usually whether the layout changes. Keeping the sink, range, and refrigerator where they are keeps costs down. Moving them opens up more options. We'll walk you through both.

</Band>

<Band>

## Bathroom remodeling

From a refreshed vanity to a full gut renovation, a bathroom remodel lives or dies on what's behind the tile. A bathroom handles water every day, so the waterproofing behind the shower walls, the slope to the drain, and the ventilation matter as much as the finishes you see.

Keeping water where it belongs is our trade as roofers. We build bathrooms with the same attention to water that we give every roof.

</Band>

<Band>

## Interior renovations

- **Flooring** throughout the house
- **Walls, trim, and doors** updated or replaced
- **Opening up rooms** by removing or reworking walls
- **Reconfiguring space** so rooms fit how you use them

Removing a load-bearing wall takes structural planning, like a beam sized to carry the load. We'll tell you which walls can come out and what it takes.

</Band>

<Band>

## Complete home renovations

A complete renovation updates the whole house as one project. You can do it room by room, so parts of the home stay usable, or all at once, which is usually faster when the house is empty.

Either way, the work has to happen in the right order: structure and anything inside the walls first, then surfaces, then finishes. We plan the sequence before work starts, so the project doesn't stall halfway through.

</Band>
