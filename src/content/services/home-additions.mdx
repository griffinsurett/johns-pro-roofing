## Addition, extension, or reworking what you have?

There's more than one way to get more space, and the right one depends on your home and your lot.

- **A room addition** builds new rooms onto the house, like a bedroom, family room, office, or suite.
- **An extension** pushes out an existing room to make it larger, such as a kitchen or living room.
- **Expanding your existing space** reworks and opens up the rooms you already have, sometimes with no new footprint at all.
- **Structural additions and renovations** change the structure itself and tie into your home's existing framing and roof.

We'll look at your home with you and help you choose the approach that gets you the most usable space for your budget.

</Band>

## Planning your addition

A few questions shape every addition before design starts:

- **How will you use the space?** A bedroom, a family room, and a kitchen extension each have different needs.
- **Where does it fit on your lot?** Local zoning rules, like setbacks from property lines, limit where and how big you can build.
- **What's under it?** The foundation for the new space needs to suit your site and your existing house.
- **How does it connect?** Heating, cooling, and electrical service need to reach the new space.

We'll talk through all of this during your free estimate, so you know what your project involves before you commit.

</Band>

## Built to match your home

An addition should look like it was always part of the house. That comes down to matching the rooflines, siding, and finishes, and tying the new structure into the old one cleanly.

The roof tie-in is the most important part. Where the new roof meets the existing one is the spot most likely to leak if it's done wrong. Roofing is our trade, so we tie the two together cleanly and keep the connection watertight.

</Band>
