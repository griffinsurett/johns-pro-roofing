## Trex composite or wood

- **Trex composite decking** resists rot, splintering, and fading, and doesn't need staining or sealing
- **Wood decking** costs less up front and has a classic look, with periodic sealing or staining to keep it protected

We'll walk you through both options, including the long-term upkeep, so you can choose what fits your budget.

## Replacements, repairs, and finishing touches

- **Deck replacements**: tear-out of a worn or unsafe deck and a new build in its place
- **Deck repairs**: soft or rotted boards, loose railings, and failing stairs
- **Railings and stairs**: new or upgraded railings and stairs that are safe and solid
- **Covered decks**: a roof over your deck for shade and rain cover, built by a company that installs roofs every day
