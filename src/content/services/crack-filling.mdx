## Small cracks, big consequences

Every crack in your asphalt is a doorway for water. Once water gets under the surface and freezes, it lifts and breaks the pavement apart — and a hairline crack you could have sealed for a little becomes a pothole that costs a lot. Crack filling is the cheapest, fastest way to protect the paving you already have.

John's Pro Roofing seals cracks for parking lots and drive lanes across New Jersey, keeping your surface intact and your property safe for the people using it.
