## What a general contractor actually does

Almost every building project needs more than one trade. There's demolition, framing, electrical, plumbing, drywall and finish work, and they have to happen in the right order. Get the order wrong and crews end up waiting on each other, or tearing out work that's already done.

A general contractor owns that whole sequence. We plan the job, schedule every trade, order materials, keep the site safe, pull the permits and schedule the inspections.

When a job's split between five separate contractors and something goes wrong, everyone points at someone else. With us there's nobody else to point at. You've got one company responsible for the schedule, the budget and the finished work, and one number to call.
