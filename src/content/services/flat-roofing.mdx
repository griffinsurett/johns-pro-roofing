## A clean tear-off and a solid deck

A flat-roof system is only as good as what's under it. On a full replacement we tear off the old, waterlogged membrane, inspect the deck, and replace any sheathing that's soft or rotted — so the new roof starts on a sound, dry surface instead of sealing problems in.

Getting the base right is what separates a roof that lasts decades from one that leaks again in a few years.

</Band>

<Band image={bandMembrane} imageAlt="Fresh flat-roof membrane installed and sealed">

## A membrane sealed for the long haul

With the deck prepped, we lay the new membrane and detail every seam, edge, and penetration to manufacturer spec. Proper detailing at the transitions is where flat roofs win or lose — so we take the time to get the flashing, drains, and terminations watertight.

The finished surface sheds water cleanly and is built to carry your building through New Jersey's freeze-thaw cycles, backed by {businessData.warranty}.
