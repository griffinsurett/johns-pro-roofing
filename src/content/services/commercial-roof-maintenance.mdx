## Maintenance that pays for itself

The most expensive roof problems are the ones nobody caught in time. A maintenance program puts your roof on a schedule — regular inspections, minor repairs, and clear reporting — so small issues get fixed for a few dollars instead of turning into five-figure emergencies and interior damage.

For property managers and facility managers juggling multiple buildings, it's one less thing to worry about: we track the condition, flag what needs attention, and keep the paperwork your warranty requires.

## What a maintenance program covers

- **Scheduled inspections** of the membrane, seams, flashing, and drains
- **Minor repairs and upkeep** handled on the spot
- **Debris and drain clearing** to prevent ponding
- **Condition reporting** with photos so you can plan budgets
- **Warranty compliance** — many manufacturer warranties require documented maintenance
