## The cheapest way to protect your lot

Sealcoating is the single most cost-effective thing you can do for a commercial parking lot. A thin protective coat blocks the water, UV, and oil that break asphalt down — so instead of resurfacing every few years, you get a lot that lasts and looks freshly paved.

For property managers watching a maintenance budget, a sealcoating cycle is easy math: a small recurring cost that pushes major paving expenses years down the road.

## What sealcoating does

- **Blocks water** from seeping into cracks and undermining the base
- **Shields against UV and oxidation** that turn asphalt gray and brittle
- **Resists oil, gas, and chemicals** that soften the surface
- **Restores a clean, uniform black finish** that signals a well-kept property
- **Pairs with line striping** for a full refresh of your lot
