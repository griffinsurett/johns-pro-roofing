A commercial convenience-store lot that had faded to gray, with cracks opening up and line striping worn nearly invisible. The crew cleaned and prepped the surface, filled the cracks, and applied a fresh sealcoat before laying down crisp new striping — parking stalls, an ADA space, and clean curb lines. The finished lot is protected from water and UV, safer to navigate, and a far stronger first impression for the business.
