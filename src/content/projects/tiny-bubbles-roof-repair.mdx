Trapped moisture had worked its way under the membrane, leaving the flat roof covered in bubbles and blisters that were pulling the seams apart and letting water in. The crew cut out the compromised sections, dried and prepped the deck, then patched and resealed the surface for a smooth, watertight finish. The repaired roof lies flat again — no more bubbling, no more leaks — protecting the building for years to come.
