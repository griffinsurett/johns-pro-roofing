A commercial parking lot that had cracked, faded, and started breaking apart under years of daily traffic. The crew removed the failing surface, corrected the grade for proper drainage, and laid down a fresh, fully compacted asphalt surface. The finished lot is smooth, clean, and built to hold up — a stronger first impression for the property and a safer surface for everyone who parks and drives on it.
