A commercial flat roof that had begun to fail — pooling water, worn membrane, and active leaks threatening the space below. Rather than a full tear-off, the crew cleaned and prepped the existing surface, repaired the compromised areas, and resealed the entire span for a weather-tight finish. The result is a restored, leak-free roof that protects the building and extends its service life at a fraction of replacement cost.
