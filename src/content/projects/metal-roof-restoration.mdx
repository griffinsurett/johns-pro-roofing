Restoration of an aging standing-seam metal roof. The existing panels had rusted through and seams were beginning to fail, so instead of a full tear-off the crew cleaned, primed, and re-sealed the surface, then sprayed a white reflective roof coating across the entire span. The finished roof is fully weather-tight, stops the leaks, and reflects heat to lower cooling costs — extending the life of the roof for years at a fraction of the cost of replacement.
