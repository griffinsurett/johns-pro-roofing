Complete re-roof on an older brick multi-family home. The crew tore off the failing shingles down to the deck, replaced worn underlayment, and installed new dark architectural shingles across the full span. The finished roof restores the character of the historic home while giving it modern weather protection and a clean, uniform look from the street.
